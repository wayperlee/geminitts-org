# GeminiTTS

Text to speech online powered by Gemini TTS — narration or two-speaker dialogue.

Homepage: https://geminitts.org/

## What it does

GeminiTTS is a browser studio for natural voiceovers with Gemini speech models (independent app, not a Google product). Pick from many voices, Flash or Flash Lite, single voice or dialogue, then download WAV.

- [GeminiTTS](https://geminitts.org/)
- Alternate domain: https://geminitts.app/
